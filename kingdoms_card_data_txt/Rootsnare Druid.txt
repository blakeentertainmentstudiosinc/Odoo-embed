Rules Text:
[Grapple]: Takes no combat damage when attacking taxed units. Units this deals combat damage to get taxed & stay taxed during their untax step if this is alive.

[Entrance]: Target a unit, this may grapple it, then you may move it to the front-line &/or tax it. If it’s taxed, this turn it takes O combat damage & is untargettable. If it’s untaxed, remove up to 2 of its counters or destroy 1 of its supplies.
