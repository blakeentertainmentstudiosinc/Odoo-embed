Rules Text:
[Entrance & Upkeep]: Reveal the top 2 cards of your main deck; you may send any units among them to the grave. The other cards go back on top in any order.

[Passive]: Mordin gets +1/+1 for every 2 units in all exiles combined. Instead of going to the grave, friendly units may be exiled with an after-life counter. You may pay 1 Gold to do the same to enemy units.

Fringe – [Once per your turn, Cost 4]: Target a unit exiled with an after-life counter & play it with an un-death counter. (Instead of dying, it’s exiled.)
