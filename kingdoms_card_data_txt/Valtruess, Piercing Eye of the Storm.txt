Rules Text:
Epic – [Entrance]: If played from the hand/throne room, you may destroy all other non-bastion cards. Starting with you & going in turn order, each player chooses up to 3 cards in grave that were destroyed this way & plays them. For each non-terra card played this way, its player searches their terra deck for a basic terra, plays it taxed, & shuffles. Then this gets a +1/+1 counter for each terra in play. (Only resolve one epic effect per turn.)

[Ranged]: If in the kingdom, may attack the front-line. If able to attack a bastion, may target its units. Only ranged units can defend themselves/their bastion from ranged.
