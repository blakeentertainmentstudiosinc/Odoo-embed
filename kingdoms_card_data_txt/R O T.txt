[Dreg]: Cannot be a royal.

[Invulnerable to]: The effects of -1/-1 counters.

Ritual – As you play ROT you MUST sacrifice a unit(s): For each, put a -1/-1 counter on ROT & reduce its cost by 1.

[Passive]: Units deal combat damage to each-other in the form of -1/-1 counters. Units leaving play transfer their -1/-1 counters to ROT. ROT’s ATK is equal to the number of -1/-1 counters it has.
